## TL;DR (answers to the core question)
- **The single most decisive fact for this applicant:** headline music **talent scholarships almost universally require declaring the music major** (Holy Cross Brooks = full tuition but music-major-only; NYU Steinhardt jazz talent awards require applying to the BM; UW–Madison's Summer Music Clinic waiver requires being a music major). **Ensembles and private lessons are open to non-majors nearly everywhere, but the money is gated behind the major.** The one profile-fit exception for a non-major who just wants to sing jazz is **UW–Eau Claire**, where jazz is fully co-curricular and open to "all Blugolds."
- **The cheapest realistic net costs for this family** are, in order: **Notre Dame** (if combined parental income lands under $150K → free tuition; ~$91,986 sticker COA but tuition wiped out), **UW–Madison / UW–Eau Claire** (Wisconsin resident tuition ~$12,416 / ~$10,207), and **Holy Cross** (meets 100% of need; full-tuition grant only if income ≤$100,000 with reasonable assets — the custodial income under $150K may exceed that cutoff). NYU (~$101,000 COA) and Fordham (~$85,000+) are the most expensive and least generous.
- **Two "Already established" items are CONTRADICTED by primary sources:** (1) Notre Dame's supplement is **one required 150-word essay + two short answers chosen from four** (not "two vs. three" — it is two, and there is no separate 100-word academics essay this cycle); (2) **DePaul's School of Music has its own December 1 deadline with no Early Action** — the Nov 15 EA applies to the general university, not to music-major applicants.

## Key Findings (10 most decision-relevant)

1. **CONTRADICTS "Already established" — Notre Dame writing supplement (RESOLVED from admissions.nd.edu):** ONE required 150-word essay + TWO short answers (50–100 words each) chosen from FOUR options. The "two vs. three short answers" question resolves to **TWO**. There is **no** separate 100-word academic-interest essay in 2026-27; blogs listing three answers or an academics essay are reporting prior cycles.

2. **Notre Dame income-threshold program ("Pathways to Notre Dame," effective 2026-27):** Per Notre Dame News (news.nd.edu, March 18, 2026), verbatim: *"families with annual income below $150,000 will receive need-based financial aid that covers the cost of tuition. Families with income below $200,000 will receive need-based aid that covers half the cost of tuition. In addition, most students from families with annual income below $60,000 will receive need-based financial aid that covers tuition, fees, housing and food."* ND is need-blind, loan-free, and meets 100% of demonstrated need. **CRITICAL for this divorced-parent household:** ND **requires the Noncustodial Parent CSS Profile**, so the noncustodial parent's income **counts** toward the $150K determination. A custodial income under $150K alone does not guarantee the benefit if combined parental income exceeds the threshold. A waiver is available via an IDOC petition only when there is genuinely no contact.

3. **Holy Cross income-threshold program:** Per the College's Oct. 9, 2024 announcement (magazine.holycross.edu), *"students whose families have a total income of $100,000 or less and reasonable assets will receive a full tuition grant"* (raised from the prior $75,000 threshold). Holy Cross meets 100% of demonstrated need. Because this family's custodial income is under $150K but possibly above $100K, the full-tuition grant is **not guaranteed** — but strong need-based aid still applies.

4. **CONTRADICTS "Already established" — DePaul:** The **School of Music has its own December 1 deadline and NO early action**; music-major decisions are released **March 1–April 1** (music.depaul.edu). The Nov 15 EA / Dec 15 decision applies to the general university only. **Jazz Studies requires a prescreen for ALL applicants**, and applicants register for a live audition by January 15 only after prescreen results.

5. **UW–Madison jazz voice prescreen drives the entire fall calendar.** For 2025-26 (PRIOR-CYCLE, from music.wisc.edu), the earliest voice/jazz prescreen was due **10/31/25 for an 11/22/25 audition**. The 2026-27 jazz and voice audition documents were **not yet posted** on music.wisc.edu as of this research. **If the pattern holds, the earliest prescreen falls on/around Oct 31, 2026 — BEFORE the Nov 1 application deadline.** UNVERIFIED for 2026-27 — CONFIRM WITH SCHOOL (admissions@music.wisc.edu).

6. **Music talent scholarships require declaring the major, per school:**
   - **Holy Cross Brooks Music Scholarship (full tuition):** requires majoring or double-majoring in music — **NOT** available to non-majors.
   - **UW–Madison Summer Music Clinic tuition waiver:** "Students must be a music major or double major with a music major to be eligible."
   - **NYU Steinhardt Jazz Studies:** talent awards automatic but require applying to the BM (a major).
   - **Non-majors are welcome in ensembles and private lessons at ND, UW–Madison, Holy Cross, UMN, and UW–Eau Claire, but are largely ineligible for the headline talent money.**

7. **NYU splits the artistic interests across two schools with separate applications, reviews, and deadlines:** Jazz voice → **Steinhardt** (BM Jazz Studies; BM Vocal Performance: Classical/Contemporary/Music Theatre), prescreen required. Musical theatre → **Tisch** (Drama; "Musical Theatre Only" → New Studio on Broadway), Drama Artistic Review required.

8. **Minnesota–Wisconsin reciprocity DOES apply to UMN–Twin Cities.** Per Minnesota OHE, a Wisconsin resident at UMN–Twin Cities pays *"the higher of the tuition charged by the University of Minnesota-Twin Cities or by the University of Wisconsin-Madison"* — in practice the UMN Minnesota-resident rate. For 2026-27: tuition & fees **$19,312**, total COA **$38,226** (add a $3,100/yr surcharge for the College of Science & Engineering or Carlson). **Apply through the Universities of Wisconsin portal (wisconsin.edu), separate from admission**; the Fall 2026 application opened Feb 1, 2026, so the Fall 2027 equivalent will open ~February 2027 (UNVERIFIED exact date).

9. **Wisconsin free-tuition programs both fall far below this family's income:** Bucky's Tuition Promise (UW–Madison) covers tuition & segregated fees for **AGI ≤ $65,000** (financialaid.wisc.edu; raised from $60,000, originally $56,000). Wisconsin Tuition Promise (all other UW campuses incl. Eau Claire) covers **AGI ≤ $55,000** (wisconsin.edu). **A custodial income under $150K qualifies for neither.** Note: beginning 2026-27, Bucky's Tuition Promise recipients must submit the CSS Profile for their incoming year.

10. **2027-28 FAFSA and CSS Profile both open by October 1, 2026** (U.S. Dept. of Education Electronic Announcement APP-26-10; a FAFSA beta is running now). First application/aid deadlines cluster at **Nov 1** (ND REA, UW–Madison EA, UMN EA I, Fordham EA/ED I, NYU ED I) with ND's aid-priority and SlideRoom arts deadline at **Nov 15**.
