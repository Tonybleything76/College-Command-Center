## Ground rules

**Essays are hers.** This project produces brainstorming scaffolds, question sets,
outlines, reverse-outlines, and revision feedback. It does not produce essay drafts.
Several of these schools require applicants to attest that their writing is their own.

**Nothing gets acted on unverified.** Every deadline, dollar figure, and prompt carries
a source and a date, or it's marked `UNVERIFIED — CONFIRM WITH SCHOOL`. Gaps are
labeled, never filled with plausible numbers.
